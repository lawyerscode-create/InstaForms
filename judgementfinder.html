About this tool

Judgement Finder is a free reference tool for Indian lawyers and legal interns to quickly spot precedent that resembles their case facts. This build runs on a curated set of landmark Indian judgments and matches them to your input using keyword relevance, entirely in your browser.

It is a starting point for research, not a substitute for verifying citations, reading full judgment text, and checking whether a precedent has since been overruled or distinguished. Always confirm on Indian Kanoon, SCC Online, Manupatra, or the relevant court's official website before relying on any case.

Good to know

  • Court and year shown are indicative — verify full citation before filing.
  • "In Favour" / "Against" assumes you're presenting facts as the party seeking relief.
  • This demo dataset covers ~30 landmark cases, not the full body of Indian case law.
  • A production version would connect to a licensed case-law database and legal-language search.
