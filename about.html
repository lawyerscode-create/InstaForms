About InstaForms

InstaForms began as a study aid — a shelf-by-shelf index of the drafts Indian law students and interns reach for most often, built so that no one has to hunt across a dozen tabs to remember what a bail application needs, or where the official rent agreement format lives.

We are not a law firm and we do not provide legal advice. InstaForms is an independent reference and drafting-aid platform: we describe what a document is for, what you'll typically need on hand before you draft it, and we point you to the official government or court source for the current format. Every description on this site is written as general guidance only — procedure, stamp duty, court fees and filing requirements vary by state and change over time, so we always link out to the current official source rather than hosting our own copies of court forms.

The site is maintained for three audiences: practising advocates who want a quick refresher, law students and interns learning the shape of a matter for the first time, and everyday clients trying to understand a document before they sign it. If something on a shelf looks outdated or the linked source has moved, please let us know — we rely on readers to help us keep the library accurate.
